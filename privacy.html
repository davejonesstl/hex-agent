Privacy policy

Hex is a single-user personal assistant. Its only user is its owner.

  • What it accesses: email, files and calendar events in the dedicated Google account the owner connects, plus any items the owner explicitly shares with that account.
  • How data is used: only to perform tasks for the owner, such as summarizing new email or managing calendar events.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens and working data are kept on the owner's private server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections settings.
